Studio

A small practice built so that the architect never leaves the room.

Plansman Inc. is an architect-led residential design and construction firm based in Rancho Santa Margarita, California. We design custom homes, additions, whole-house remodels, accessory dwelling units and outdoor living spaces, and we engineer, permit and, when asked, build them.

The firm started as a drafting service and grew into a licensed architecture and engineering practice with construction in-house. That history shows in how we work: drawings that contractors can build from, structure resolved with the design rather than after it, and plan-check comments answered quickly because the person who drew the set is the one answering.

Every project is personally led by Lance Higgins, Principal Architect, supported by an experienced, coordinated team. The result is a streamlined process with clear communication, efficient execution and fewer delays.

Services

How a project runs

Six steps, in plain language, from the first conversation to a permit in hand.

  1. Conversation

    Tell us about the property and what you want from it. We respond quickly, ask the questions that matter, and give you a ballpark fee so you can decide whether to go further.

  2. Site consultation

    The architect visits the site, measures and photographs existing conditions, reviews your documents and talks through the opportunities and the permitting path. You receive a firm proposal afterward.

  3. Design

    We develop the design with you in a few focused rounds, from layouts and massing to renderings, until it is right. You approve the design before any construction drawings begin.

  4. Documents & engineering

    Architectural and structural drawings are produced together as one coordinated set, ready for submittal.

  5. Permit

    We submit to the city or county, respond to plan-check comments, and keep going until the permit is in hand.

  6. Build

    Build with Plansman, or hand a complete, permitted set to the contractor of your choice. Either way, your architect is a phone call away through construction.

Fees are proposed in writing after the site consultation and are typically split between the start of design and the completion of the permit set. Additional services, such as added scope or extra agency submittals, are quoted before any work begins.
