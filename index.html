How a project runs

The full process

A clear path from the first conversation to a permitted set, written for homeowners rather than for other architects.

  1. Site consultation

    The architect visits the site, measures and photographs existing conditions, reviews your documents and talks through the opportunities and the permitting path. You receive a firm proposal afterward.

  2. Design

    We develop the design with you in a few focused rounds, from layouts and massing to renderings, until it is right. You approve the design before any construction drawings begin.

  3. Documents & engineering & permit

    One coordinated architectural and structural set, submitted to the city and carried through plan check until the permit is issued.

  4. Build

    Build with Plansman, or hand a complete, permitted set to the contractor of your choice. Either way, your architect is a phone call away through construction.

Services

  1. Architecture

    Design from the first sketch through construction documents, led personally by a licensed architect. Site plans, floor plans, elevations, details and the revisions it takes to get them approved.

  2. Structural Engineering

    Foundations, framing and lateral design produced alongside the architecture, so the structure is resolved with the design instead of after it.

  3. Permitting

    We prepare the submittal, answer the plan checker, and carry the set through Planning and Building until the permit is issued.

  4. Construction

    For clients who want one team start to finish, Plansman builds what it draws.
