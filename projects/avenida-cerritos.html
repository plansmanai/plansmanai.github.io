Avenida Cerritos

Newport Beach, California

Location
Newport Beach, California
Type
Whole-House Remodel
Services
Architecture, Structural Engineering, Permitting
Status
Completed

An Orange County developer purchased this investment property above the Back Bay. The whole residence was gutted, walls were moved, and a new layout was drawn so the water view reaches the kitchen, dining room and living room at once.

Bi-fold doors erase the line between the living level and the terrace. The home sold at a premium shortly after completion.
