Hollywood Hills Residence

Los Angeles, California

Location
Los Angeles, California
Type
Custom Home
Services
Architecture (with Gasparoly & Watson), Construction Documents
Status
Completed

A collaboration with Gasparoly & Watson, this three-story custom home steps down a steep Hollywood Hills lot. The plan is a series of stacked, cantilevered volumes, each one framing the city below.

A rooftop pool deck sits level with the main living floor. Inside, a skylit stair, a red screening room and a glass-walled primary bath carry the same uncompromising idea: clean planes, big openings, and the view as the finish material.
