Bleriot Avenue

Westchester, Los Angeles

Location
Westchester, Los Angeles
Type
Addition & Remodel
Services
Architecture, Structural Engineering, Permitting
Status
Completed

A Los Angeles developer bought this post-war bungalow near LAX with three goals: add square footage, renovate the interior, and upgrade the landscape. We rebuilt the street face in dark board-and-batten with a new garage and a crisp paver drive.

Inside, the plan opens to a new kitchen and living room with vaulted ceilings, and a rear addition brings the bedrooms out to the yard.
