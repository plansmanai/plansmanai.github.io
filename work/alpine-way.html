Alpine Way

Laguna Beach, California

Location
Laguna Beach, California
Type
Whole-House Remodel
Services
Architecture, Structural Engineering, Permitting
Status
Completed

This single-family residence on a Laguna Beach hillside had the view and little else. We took it down to the studs, reconfigured the rooms so every main space faces the canyon and ocean, and upgraded the appointments throughout.

Bi-fold glass walls open the kitchen and living room to a new deck. A loft above the living room borrows the same view. The palette stays quiet, white oak, honed marble and black steel, so the landscape does the talking.

Architecture and engineering by Plansman Inc.
