Summit Court

Las Flores, Orange County

Location
Las Flores, Orange County
Type
Pool & Outdoor Living
Services
Architecture, Structural Engineering, Permitting
Status
Completed

The site was a sloped backyard of old rosebushes in the hills of south Orange County. The owners wanted somewhere to swim, sit and host that felt classic rather than trendy.

A long pool and raised spa anchor the terrace, faced in blue-and-white patterned tile with a sheet waterfall. Limestone decks, a shaded lounge and a lawn complete a sequence of outdoor rooms.

Architecture and engineering by Plansman Inc.
