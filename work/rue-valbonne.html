Rue Valbonne

Newport Beach, California

Location
Newport Beach, California
Type
Addition & Whole-House Remodel
Services
Architecture, Structural Engineering, Permitting
Status
Completed

An Orange County developer bought this classic Newport Beach house intent on upgrading every inch of it. The brief was simple to say and hard to do: more light, more connection to the outdoors, and a plan that works for how people actually live.

We expanded the kitchen into a single open living level, reorganized the upstairs around a glass-railed stair, and gave the exterior a clean, quiet aesthetic of white brick, black steel windows and warm wood. A sequence of terraces and courtyards turns a tight coastal lot into a series of outdoor rooms.

Architecture and engineering by Plansman Inc.
