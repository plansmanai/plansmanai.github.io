Laguna Beach Ranch

Laguna Beach, California

Location
Laguna Beach, California
Type
Custom Home & Guest House
Services
Architecture, Structural Engineering, Permitting
Status
In design

The owner of a successful surf business commissioned the demolition and reconstruction of his family's primary residence. The brief: a modern take on the classic California ranch, a guest house, and places to entertain outside.

Three gabled pavilions in standing-seam metal and warm cedar step across the site, linked by a pool court and covered terraces. The renderings below show the design as it heads into construction documents.
